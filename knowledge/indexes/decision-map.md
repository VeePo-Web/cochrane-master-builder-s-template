# Backend Decision Map

When an AI assistant (Lovable, Claude Code, Antigravity, etc.) is asked to make a design, UX, or structural decision, it MUST use this map to find the appropriate backend intelligence file to filter its output.

### ⭐️ Ultimate Brand Identity North Star (Final Arbiter)
- **Consult Target:** `1.5_Brand_Identity_North_Star_Report_Partner.md`
- **Output Goal:** Override any modern, fast, or cheap design trends. Force all output to conform to the ultimate philosophical boundaries of the brand: untempered luxury, generational durability, and elite, authoritative tradition.

### 🧠 Global Conversion Psychology & Trust Architecture
- **Consult Target:** `1.4_Deep_ICP_Psychology_Report_Partner.md`
- **Output Goal:** Establish the macro emotional triggers (heritage, elite craftsmanship, ultimate safety) that govern all global layouts, testimonials, and high-ticket service setups before segmenting into specific personas.

### 🧭 Navigation & Footer (UI / UX)
- **Consult Target:** `1.2.1_Family_Legacy_Standard_Partner.md`
- **Output Goal:** Ensure Apple-level cleanliness, bespoke interaction, and strict alignment with the family-owned legacy brand. No generic bootsrap styles.

### 🎨 Visual & Motion Polish (Hover States, Layouts, Micro-interactions)
- **Consult Targets:** `1.2.1_Family_Legacy_Standard_Partner.md`, `1.3.1_Bespoke_Traditional_UX_Design_Phase_Report_Partner.md`, AND `1.5.1_Volume_2_Website_Ux_Ui_Psychology_System_Partner.md`
- **Output Goal:** Achieve `fantasy.co` and `igloo.inc` quality levels. Enforce the "Bespoke Traditional UX Design" standard using Apple-level interaction. Apply the 1.5.1 UX system to ensure motion, easing curves, and physical button friction perfectly evoke the legacy North Star aesthetics.

### 🔨 Sub-Company Creation (e.g., Cochrane Tile Masters, Drywall Masters)
- **Consult Target:** `1.2.1_Family_Legacy_Standard_Partner.md`
- **Output Goal:** Inherit the main Master Builders brand identity, but specialize the copy and imagery for the precise sub-service. Remove any non-relevant artifacts (e.g., "weddings") and refine it entirely for the VeePo construction ecosystem.

### 📈 SEO, Page Structure & Conversion Psychology
- **Consult Targets:** `1.0_Strategic_Business_SEO_UX_Report_Partner.md`, `1.2_Strategic_Business_SEO_UX_Report_Partner.md`, AND `1.5.2_Volume_3_Seo_Ai_Seo_Service_Ecosystem_Partner.md`
- **Output Goal:** Ensure semantic HTML, proper meta hierarchies, structured flows for lead generation, and optimal SEO architecture designed for local dominance. Use Volume 3 (1.5.2) specifically to construct advanced JSON-LD schema and localized Sub-service interlinking.

### ✍️ Copywriting, Tone & Emotional Trust
- **Consult Targets:** `1.2.2_Foundations_For_Generations_After_Us_Report_Partner.md` AND `1.4.3_Grandfathers_ICP_UX_Report_Partner.md`
- **Output Goal:** Craft messaging and "About Us" layouts that reflect a highly trustworthy, generational legacy brand. Avoid modern abstraction. Ensure typography is readable, authoritative, and anchored in elite craftsmanship.

### ⚙️ Operating Standards, Guarantees & Process Workflows
- **Consult Target:** `1.5.3_Volume_4_Family_Legacy_Business_Operating_Standard_Partner.md`
- **Output Goal:** Dictate the procedural logic for "How it Works" sections, contact form post-submission messaging, and the ironclad professional warranties attached to the brand. Ensure operations sound deeply communicative and impeccably structured.

### 🕸️ Holistic Architecture: Bridging SEO, Design & Legacy
- **Consult Target:** `1.3_Backend_Strategy_Design_SEO_Legacy_Report_Partner.md`
- **Output Goal:** Ensure that foundational backend choices (like navigation linking, semantic tags, and component structure) perfectly support BOTH the rigorous SEO strategy and the elite `fantasy.co` aesthetic without compromising either.

### 👷 B2B Funnels & Subcontractor Recruitment
- **Consult Target:** `1.4.1_Subcontractor_ICP_UX_Report_Partner.md`
- **Output Goal:** Design the UX, flow, and copywriting for Partner/Careers pages to specifically convert high-end subcontractors. Remove residential fluff and focus on efficiency, legacy, and reliability.

### 🏡 Residential UX, Safety & Maternal Trust
- **Consult Target:** `1.4.2_Mothers_ICP_UX_Report_Partner.md`
- **Output Goal:** Structure consumer-facing residential pages, contact forms, and process timelines to alleviate friction points regarding mess and safety. Output must feel impeccably clean, empathetic, and strictly premium.

### ⚔️ Competitor Positioning & AI-SEO Markup
- **Consult Target:** `1.1_Market_Competitor_AI_SEO_Research_Report_Partner.md`
- **Output Goal:** Inject highly aggressive but elegant AI-optimized keywords into body copy, structure rich schema markups, and construct "Why Choose Us" sections that explicitly neutralize market competitors.

### 💼 Conversion, CTA Design, Forms, Lead Qualification & CRM
- **Consult Target:** `1.5.5_Volume_6_Conversion_CRM_Proof_Governance_System_Partner.md`
- **Output Goal:** All CTAs must follow the three-tier hierarchy (Primary: Get Your Free Estimate / Secondary: View Work / Tertiary: Trust anchors). All forms must use progressive disclosure architecture. Lead routing must map to the 15-stage CRM pipeline. Follow-up cadences, proposal structure ("Investment Summary" framing), review generation triggers, and proof library taxonomy are all governed by this document. Never use generic SaaS-style button copy or single-page flat forms.

### 🏆 Proof, Testimonials, Reviews & Reputation Management
- **Consult Target:** `1.5.5_Volume_6_Conversion_CRM_Proof_Governance_System_Partner.md`
- **Output Goal:** Structure testimonial sections, case study pages, and review widgets to match the Project Story System format and Proof Library taxonomy defined in Volume 6. Review solicitation must follow the post-project milestone trigger sequence. Negative review handling must use the Reputation Risk/Recovery protocol — never improvise a response to a negative review.

### 📊 Analytics, KPIs & Conversion Governance
- **Consult Target:** `1.5.5_Volume_6_Conversion_CRM_Proof_Governance_System_Partner.md`
- **Output Goal:** Any analytics integration, reporting dashboard, or performance tracking feature must align to the KPI definitions and governance cadence (weekly/monthly review thresholds) specified in Volume 6. Do not invent new metrics that contradict or duplicate the defined conversion benchmarks.
