## 2. What this document is

The **backend-aware** evolution of the strategy line. Where v1.0 and v1.2 articulate CMB as a single brand, v1.3 articulates how CMB operates as a **system of sister trade sites** with shared backend, shared brand DNA, shared SEO moat, and inherited design language — while each site stays bespoke at the front end. Also frames the legacy / generational positioning at an operational level, not just a copy level.

## 3. Decision triggers — when to consult this doc

- Designing or evolving the multi-trade taxonomy in `src/master/trades.ts`.
- Deciding what lives in `src/master/` (parent) vs. `src/config/trade.config.ts` (per-remix).
- Backlink network strategy across sister sites (`src/master/seo/backlink-network.ts`).
- Footer architecture that references the parent CMB network.
- Legal / warranty framing that has to work across all trades.
- Trust signals at scale (license, insurance, NAP, GBP) — how they propagate from master to remix.
- "Should this go in the brand bible or the trade config?" questions.

## 4. Decision types this doc DOES NOT govern

- Single-trade hero copy or single-page UX → `ux-design/1.3.1.partner.md` + relevant persona.
- Voice and tagline → `brand-identity/1.2.x.partner.md`.
- Keyword research itself (vs. cross-site keyword distribution) → `seo-research/1.1.partner.md`.
- Persona empathy on a specific audience → `personas-icp/1.4.x.partner.md`.

## 5. How to read it

- Parse PDF first via `document--parse_document`.
- Start with the "system architecture" / multi-trade section.
- Then read the legacy / generational framing as it applies to backend (e.g. "what survives a 50-year horizon?").
- For SEO, focus on the inter-site network section, not the keyword tactics.

## 6. Routing precedence

- **Wins over `strategy/1.0` and `strategy/1.2`** on any multi-trade, sister-site, backend, or system-level question.
- **Loses** to `seo-research/1.1` on tactical keywords and to `ux-design/1.3.1` on within-page layout decisions.
- Wins over `brand-identity/*` only on questions of *system propagation* (how brand inherits from master to remix), not on the brand content itself.
