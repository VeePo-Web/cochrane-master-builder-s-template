# Universal Website Copywriting Template Plan — Partner Brief (Foundational, Copy Axis)

**Status:** FOUNDATIONAL — **COPY axis**. Sits *on top of* the Universal Website Template Wireframe Plan (structural axis). The wireframe defines section *positions*; this defines section *words*. Together with brand-identity v1.2.1 / v1.2.2 and UX v1.3.1, these form the foundational layer every Cochrane spin-off site is rendered through.

**Source of truth:**

- `source-documents/brands/cochrane-master-builders/strategy/universal_website_copywriting_template_plan.source.md` (verbatim text mirror)

## The One Customer Question

Every line of copy must move the visitor toward answering YES to:

> **"Can I trust this company with something my family, property, money, or future depends on?"**

This is the actual conversion problem. Materials, square footage, schedule are downstream.

---

## Master Narrative Backbone (every page)

1. **Problem** — they have a project AND uncertainty
2. **Empathy** — construction is stressful; opening up home, budget, schedule, trust
3. **Insight** — good construction is about what happens *years later*, not the finished photo
4. **Transformation** — uncertainty → clarity (process, pricing, scope, next step)
5. **Proof** — process, services, prices, locality, before/after, FAQs, photos, family-owned accountability

---

## Master Voice Anchor

> **Strong Foundations For Those Who Come After Us.**

MUST appear on every site, in some form, in at least 4 surfaces (hero subtext, legacy block, footer, about, process — pick 4+). Service-adapted variants are fine; the spine is non-negotiable.

### Voice attributes
Clear · Strong · Grounded · Humble · Practical · Emotionally intelligent · Family-first · Neighbourly · Premium but not arrogant · Confident but not loud.

### Tone by section
- Hero — clear, strong, trust-building
- Pricing — honest, practical, explanatory
- Process — calming, organized, reassuring
- Services — specific, useful, scannable
- Legacy block — emotional, sincere, humble
- Areas served — local, confident, SEO-aware
- FAQ — direct, helpful, no fluff
- Contact — reassuring, simple, low-pressure

## Always / Avoid Lists

**Always lean on:** Family-owned · Local Cochrane team · Clear pricing guidance · Built for long-term value · Neighbour-focused service · Strong workmanship · Safe, durable, practical · Protect your home · Built for the people who come after us · Request a clear estimate.

**Avoid (banned or rationed):** Best · Cheapest · Luxury (on every page) · World-class (in customer-facing copy) · Guaranteed (unless legally true) · Overhyped claims · Vague *quality / excellence / solutions* without proof · Hard-sell language · Generic SEO-contractor phrasing · Automotive-editorial vocabulary borrowed from VeePo.

**Strong-rewrite pattern (from source):**
- Weak: "We offer professional renovation services."
  Better: "We help Cochrane families renovate with clear pricing, careful planning, and workmanship built to hold up years after the dust settles."
- Weak: "Contact us today."
  Better: "Tell us what you are planning. We will help you understand the scope, rough range, and best next step."
- Weak: "High-quality construction."
  Better: "Work built carefully enough that we would be proud if our own grandchildren inherited it."

## Adaptation Rules

1. The narrative arc is fixed. Section order on the homepage is fixed by the wireframe; copy must respect both.
2. Master message echo: minimum 4 surfaces per site.
3. Variables, not hard-coded names — every line should remain remix-safe.
4. Pricing copy must use real v2.0 ranges + surface compliance guardrails verbatim.
5. Banned-word check is mandatory before any copy ships.
6. Voice attributes are non-negotiable — calm > clever; honest > hyped.
7. Automotive firewall — VeePo's dark-editorial register stays out.
8. Source PDF immutable.
