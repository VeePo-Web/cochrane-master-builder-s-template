## 2. What this document is

The first full strategic articulation of CMB as a business: who it serves, what business it is in, the North Star, and the highest-level SEO + UX implications that flow from that positioning. Treat it as the **constitutional layer** of strategy — broad, foundational, deliberately abstract.

## 3. Decision triggers — when to consult this doc

- Defining or restating CMB's North Star or core mission on any page.
- Answering "what business is CMB really in?" for an About, hero, or founder section.
- Choosing the top-level promise / 3-second pitch on any home hero.
- Justifying a major IA decision (e.g. "should we even have this section?") at the constitutional level.
- Deciding whether a feature, page, or trade remix is on-mission or off-mission.
- Resolving disputes about brand purpose between two partner docs that both seem valid.

## 4. Decision types this doc DOES NOT govern

- Tactical SEO keywords or local areas list → `seo-research/1.1.partner.md`.
- Specific tone / voice arrays → `brand-identity/1.2.1.partner.md` and `1.2.2.partner.md`.
- Layout, density, type scale → `ux-design/1.3.1.partner.md`.
- Persona empathy / objection handling → `personas-icp/1.4.x.partner.md`.
- Multi-trade backend taxonomy → `strategy/1.3.partner.md`.
- Tagline candidates → `brand-identity/1.2.2.partner.md`.

## 5. How to read it

- Always parse the PDF first via `document--parse_document` (binary, no shell read).
- Start with the executive summary / North Star section.
- Then jump to whichever functional pillar (Business / SEO / UX) matches your decision.
- For positioning-level decisions, read the "who we serve" and "what we promise" sections together — they only make sense as a pair.

- This doc **wins** over personas, SEO research, and UX design docs when the question is "is this on-mission for CMB at all?".
