## 2. What this document is

The **second pass** at the same strategic territory as v1.0, with refined positioning, sharper SEO direction, and updated UX implications. When v1.0 and v1.2 disagree, v1.2 is the current truth — v1.0 is kept for context on how the thinking evolved.

## 3. Decision triggers — when to consult this doc

- Any positioning decision where you'd otherwise consult v1.0 — read this one too, and let it override.
- Refining the home hero pitch / sub-hero promise after the first draft.
- Deciding how to differentiate CMB from local competitors at a strategic (not tactical-SEO) level.
- Pricing transparency framing and how openly to show ranges.
- Choosing which "pillars" deserve their own top-level page vs. a sub-section.
- Resolving any "what does CMB actually stand for *now*?" question.
