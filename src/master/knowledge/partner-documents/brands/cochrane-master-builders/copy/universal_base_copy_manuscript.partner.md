## §6 · Process (5 Steps)

**Section intro (30–50 words):**
Most homeowners don't need surprises — they need a path. Here's exactly how we move from your first call to a finished project, and what we'll need from you at each step.

1. **Step 1 — First Conversation**
   We listen first. Tell us what you're planning, what's worrying you, and what your timeline looks like. We'll tell you honestly whether we're the right fit.

2. **Step 2 — Scope Review**
   We walk the site (in person or by photos), map the scope, and flag anything we'd want to look at twice — drainage, structure, age of materials, access.

3. **Step 3 — Clear Estimate And Options**
   You get a written estimate with a real range, options where they exist, and the trade-offs explained in plain language.

4. **Step 4 — Build**
   A named project lead, a clean site, daily updates if you want them, and respect for the fact you still live in the home.

5. **Step 5 — Walkthrough And Warranty**
   Final walkthrough, paperwork, warranty notes, and a quick check-back after the first season so we catch anything before you do.

## §10 · FAQ (8 starter Q&A)

**Section intro:** Quick answers to what most Cochrane homeowners ask before they call.

1. **How quickly can you start?**
   Most [Service] projects can be scoped within a week and scheduled within a few weeks, depending on season and material lead times.

2. **Do you give written estimates?**
   Always. Verbal numbers help no one — you'll get a written scope, range, and inclusions before any work is booked.

3. **What affects the final price?**
   Scope, materials chosen, site access, structural surprises behind the walls, and seasonal demand. We walk through all of it before you commit.

4. **Are you insured and licensed?**
   Yes — full liability, WCB, and the trade tickets the work requires. Paperwork available on request.

5. **Do you handle permits?**
   For work that requires them, yes. We coordinate with the relevant authority and keep you in the loop.

6. **What areas do you serve?**
   Cochrane, Bearspaw, Springbank, Bragg Creek, Redwood Meadows, Canmore, and the Calgary west side. If you're nearby, ask — we'll tell you honestly.

7. **What's your warranty?**
   We stand behind the workmanship and pass through the manufacturer warranties on materials. Specifics are written into your estimate.

8. **What if something goes wrong after the job?**
   Call us. We do a check-back after the first season and we don't disappear once the invoice is paid.
