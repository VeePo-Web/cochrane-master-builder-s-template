# Partner Doc — Subcontractor ICP + UX Report (v1.4.1)

## 1. Source pointer

- **Source path:** `src/master/knowledge/source-documents/brands/cochrane-master-builders/personas-icp/1.4.1_Cochrane_Master_Builders_Subcontractor_ICP_UX_Report.docx`
- **Brand:** Cochrane Master Builders
- **Category:** Personas / ICP (B2B — trade vendors)
- **Format:** DOCX (binary — parse with `document--parse_document`)
- **Version:** 1.4.1

## 2. What this document is

The **subcontractor ideal-customer profile** — who CMB wants to partner with on the trade side (framers, electricians, plumbers, roofers, drywallers, etc.), what they care about, what they fear, what makes them say yes to a partnership, and the UX implications for any partner-facing flow (signup, vetting, scheduling, payment expectations).

## 3. Decision triggers — when to consult this doc

- Designing or copywriting a subcontractor / trade-vendor signup or onboarding flow.
- Partner-portal UX decisions (dashboard, document upload, license/insurance verification).
- B2B copy on a "Work with us" or "Partners" page.
- Form labels, field count, and step structure for vendor-facing forms.
- Trust signals aimed at trade professionals (license verification, insurance, payment terms).
- Email and notification copy aimed at subcontractors.
- Any decision that asks "would a busy tradesperson tap this on a job site?"

## 4. Decision types this doc DOES NOT govern

- Homeowner / family-facing copy → `personas-icp/1.4.2.partner.md` (mothers) or `1.4.3.partner.md` (grandfathers).
- Brand voice at the master level → `brand-identity/1.2.x.partner.md` (this doc adapts that voice for B2B; it does not override it).
- Layout system defaults → `ux-design/1.3.1.partner.md`.
- Multi-trade backend strategy → `strategy/1.3.partner.md`.

## 5. How to read it

- Parse DOCX first via `document--parse_document`.
- Start with the ICP profile (who this person is, day-in-the-life, motivations, fears).
- Then jump to the UX implications section for the specific flow you're designing.
- For copy register, look for the "language they actually use" / "language to avoid" section.

## 6. Routing precedence

- **Wins** on B2B / subcontractor / trade-vendor copy register, form design for vendors, and partner-portal UX.
- **Loses** to `brand-identity/1.2.x` on the master voice (B2B copy still has to sound like CMB — just adapted).
- **Loses** to `ux-design/1.3.1` on system-level layout defaults (this doc adapts those defaults for vendor flows).
- **Wins over** `personas-icp/1.4.2` and `1.4.3` on any decision involving a trade-vendor user — they govern homeowners, this one governs vendors.
