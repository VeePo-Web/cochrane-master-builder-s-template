## 2. What this document is

The **grandfather-persona ideal-customer profile** — the older homeowner (often a grandfather, sometimes a long-tenured family patriarch) who values longevity, plainspoken honesty, demonstrated craft, in-person trust signals, larger touch targets, and language that respects experience without condescending. Often making decisions on behalf of a multi-generational household or a legacy property. Includes the UX implications: type size, density, motion restraint, contact options, and trust framing.

## 3. Decision triggers — when to consult this doc

- Copy aimed at long-tenured homeowners, legacy properties, or multi-generational households.
- Type-size and contrast decisions when defaults feel marginal.
- Touch-target sizing on mobile (defaults may need to be larger for this persona).
- Motion restraint — when to dial animation back to near zero.
- Phone-call CTAs vs. form CTAs — this persona often prefers a phone number visible.
- Trust signals weighted toward longevity (years in business, founder presence, hand-shake language).
- Plainspoken copy register — no jargon, no clever wordplay.
- Legal / warranty pages where this persona is a likely reader.
- Any "would a 70-year-old patriarch trust this in 5 seconds?" sanity check.

## 5. How to read it

- Parse PDF first via `document--parse_document`.

- Then jump to the UX implications — accessibility defaults, touch sizing, type sizing, motion.
- For copy register, find the "plainspoken" / "no jargon" section and the "trust language" section.
- For contact-method preferences, find the phone-vs-form section.
