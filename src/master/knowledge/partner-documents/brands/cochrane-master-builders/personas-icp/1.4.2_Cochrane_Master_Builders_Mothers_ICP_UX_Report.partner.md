## 2. What this document is

The **mother-persona ideal-customer profile** — the family decision-maker (often but not exclusively the mother) who is researching builders, comparing trust signals, evaluating safety of contractors entering the home, scheduling around kids and routines, and weighing pricing transparency against perceived risk. Includes the UX implications: how she scans a page, what makes her bounce, what makes her book, and how she wants to be talked to.

## 3. Decision triggers — when to consult this doc

- Hero, sub-hero, or service-page copy aimed at a family decision-maker.
- Pricing transparency framing (how openly to show ranges, what to disclose first).
- Safety-of-contractor signals (license, insurance, background, "men in your home" sensitivity).
- Scheduling UX (calendar, time windows, child-routine sensitivity).
- Reviews / testimonials selection — which voices resonate with this persona.
- Booking flow copy and field count.
- Objection handling around mess, disruption, kids in the house, pets.
- Any "how does this read to a busy parent on a phone at 9pm?" sanity check.

## 5. How to read it

- Parse DOCX first via `document--parse_document`.
- Start with the persona profile (who she is, day-in-the-life, fears, triggers).
- Then jump to the UX implications for the specific surface you're designing.
- For copy register, find the "language she trusts" / "language she distrusts" section.
- For scheduling and pricing, find those dedicated subsections — they're the highest-conversion levers.

## 6. Routing precedence

- **Wins** on copy register, objection handling, scheduling UX, pricing transparency, and trust-signal *selection* (which signals she actually values), for any homeowner-facing surface where the family decision-maker is primary.
- **Loses** to `brand-identity/1.2.x` on the master voice that wraps her empathy.
- **Loses** to `ux-design/1.3.1` on layout / density / type scale.
- **Co-governs** with `personas-icp/1.4.3` on multi-generational households — when both apply, write to the more cautious of the two and verify the other isn't alienated.
