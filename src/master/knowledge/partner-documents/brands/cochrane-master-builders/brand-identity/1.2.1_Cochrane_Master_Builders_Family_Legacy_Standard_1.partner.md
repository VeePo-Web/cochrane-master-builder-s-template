## 2. What this document is

The **brand identity standard** for CMB's family-legacy positioning. It governs the *language* of family, generations, craft passed down, and what it means to "build for those who come after us." This is the source of truth for tone words, voice guardrails, and the visual register that supports the legacy positioning.

## 3. Decision triggers — when to consult this doc

- Choosing tone words / voice arrays in `src/config/trade.config.ts`.
- Deciding whether a piece of copy uses family / generational language correctly (or sounds saccharine).
- Color palette derivation from the master CMB palette to a remix accent.
- Type-pair selection that has to honor the heritage register.
- Founder / About copy.
- Reviews and testimonials framing — which voices to elevate.
- Any "is this on-brand?" check at the visual or verbal level.

## 4. Decision types this doc DOES NOT govern

- Tagline candidates and mission-statement phrasing → `brand-identity/1.2.2.partner.md` (the "Foundations" report is the tagline source).
- Layout density and component-level UX → `ux-design/1.3.1.partner.md`.
- Strategic positioning at the business level → `strategy/1.0/1.2.partner.md`.
- Tactical SEO meta titles → `seo-research/1.1.partner.md` (then wrapped in this doc's voice).

## 5. How to read it

- Parse PDF first via `document--parse_document`.
- Start with the voice / tone section.
- Then read the visual register (palette, type philosophy) section.
- For palette derivation: find the master palette spec, then apply the remix-accent rules to derive a trade-specific palette.

## 6. Routing precedence

- **Wins** on voice / tone / family-language / palette derivation / type register.
- **Loses to `1.2.2`** on tagline + mission-statement phrasing (1.2.2 is the iterated articulation of the same standard with explicit tagline territory).
- Wins over personas on *how* something is said; personas win on *what* a specific audience needs to hear.
