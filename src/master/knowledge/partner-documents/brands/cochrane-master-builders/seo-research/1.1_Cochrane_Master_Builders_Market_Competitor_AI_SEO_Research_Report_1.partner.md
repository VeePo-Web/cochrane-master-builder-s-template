## 2. What this document is

The **tactical SEO + market intelligence** layer: keyword research, competitor analysis, SERP intent for relevant queries, and AI-search visibility (LLM citations, AI Overviews, ChatGPT/Perplexity discoverability). Also the seed source for the Areas-We-Serve coverage list and competitor framing at a tactical level.

## 3. Decision triggers — when to consult this doc

- Choosing meta titles, meta descriptions, H1s, or slugs for any page.
- Building or expanding the Areas-We-Serve list (`src/master/seo/service-areas.ts`).
- Deciding which long-tail queries deserve their own dedicated page.
- Writing FAQ blocks (questions sourced from real SERP "People Also Ask").
- Planning content for AI-search visibility (citation-ready phrasing, structured answers).
- Differentiating CMB from a *named* competitor at the page level.
- Choosing JSON-LD schema density for a given page type.
- Naming services in a way that matches search intent, not internal jargon.

## 4. Decision types this doc DOES NOT govern

- Brand voice / tone of the SEO copy → `brand-identity/1.2.x.partner.md`.
- Whether a page should exist on-mission → `strategy/1.0.partner.md` or `strategy/1.2.partner.md`.
- Layout of an Areas-We-Serve page → `ux-design/1.3.1.partner.md`.
- Persona-specific search queries (e.g. how a grandfather phrases a search vs. a mother) → cross-reference the matching `personas-icp/1.4.x.partner.md`.

## 5. How to read it

- Read directly via `code--view` (this is a 1900+ line markdown file — request specific line ranges instead of dumping the whole thing).
- Start with the executive summary, then jump to the keyword cluster relevant to your page.
- For Areas-We-Serve work, find the local-area section; it includes neighborhoods and intent signals.
- For AI-search work, find the AI-SEO / LLM-citation section.

- **Wins over every other doc** for: keyword choice, meta title/description, slug, schema selection, Areas-We-Serve seed list, and AI-search visibility.
- **Loses** to `brand-identity/1.2.x` on the actual *wording* of titles and meta descriptions (SEO research dictates the keyword + structure; brand identity dictates the voice that wraps it).
- **Loses** to `strategy/1.3` on cross-site backlink network strategy.
