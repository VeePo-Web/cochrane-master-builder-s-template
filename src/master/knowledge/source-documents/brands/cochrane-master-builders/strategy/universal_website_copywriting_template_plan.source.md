# Cochrane Master Builders
## Universal Website Copywriting Template Plan

---

## Purpose Of This Copywriting Template

This document is the master copywriting system for the Cochrane Master Builders website
ecosystem.

It is designed to sit directly on top of the universal wireframe system and give every section of
every service website a clear copy strategy, message structure, section purpose, emotional
goal, customer psychology angle, and reusable copy formula.

The goal is not to create generic contractor copy.

The goal is to create a repeatable, high-converting, emotionally grounded, locally specific
copywriting framework that can support all service websites under the Cochrane Master
Builders umbrella while still allowing each service domain to feel custom.

Every site should feel:

- Local
- Family-owned
- Trustworthy
- Clear
- Premium but humble
- Long-term
- Neighbour-oriented
- Built for Cochrane families
- Practical enough to convert
- Emotional enough to be remembered

The master brand idea should guide every page:

**Strong Foundations For Those Who Come After Us.**

This line is not just a slogan. It is the narrative spine of the entire website ecosystem.

---

## Core Copywriting Principle

Every piece of copy should answer one customer question:
**Can I trust this company with something my family, property, money, or future depends on?**

That is the true conversion problem.

The customer is not only looking for roofing, siding, tile, concrete, decks, framing, basement
suites, landscaping, or renovations.

They are looking for certainty.

They want to know:

- Will this company show up?
- Will they explain the cost?
- Will they respect my home?
- Will the work last?
- Will they leave me with hidden problems?
- Will my family be safer after this?
- Is this a contractor I can call again?
- Will I regret hiring them?

The copy must reduce that anxiety section by section.

---

## Master Narrative Backbone

Every page should quietly follow this story arc:

### 1. Problem

The customer has a project, but they also have uncertainty.

They may know what they want, but they do not know who to trust, what it should cost, how long
it should take, what can go wrong, or whether the work will age well.

### 2. Empathy

The copy must acknowledge that construction can feel stressful.

A homeowner is opening up their home, budget, schedule, and trust to a contractor. That is a
serious decision.

### 3. Insight
The key insight is that good construction is not only about the finished photo.

It is about what happens years later.

The best work protects the family, holds value, prevents future problems, and becomes part of
the home’s long-term foundation.

### 4. Transformation

The customer moves from uncertainty to clarity.

They go from worrying about the project to understanding the process, pricing, scope, and next
step.

They feel confident because the company is local, family-owned, organized, and built around a
long-term standard.

### 5. Proof

The copy should support claims with:

- Clear process
- Service details
- Pricing ranges
- Local area references
- Before/after examples
- FAQs
- Photos
- Project explanations
- Brand values
- Centralized inquiry system
- Family-owned accountability

---

## Master Voice And Tone

The voice should sound like a highly competent local builder who is calm, honest, experienced,
and protective.

The copy should not sound like a generic SEO contractor website.

It should not feel like a hard sell.
It should feel like a steady local expert walking the homeowner through the decision.

### Voice Attributes

- Clear
- Strong
- Grounded
- Humble
- Practical
- Emotionally intelligent
- Family-first
- Neighbourly
- Premium but not arrogant
- Confident but not loud

### Tone By Section

Hero: clear, strong, trust-building.

Pricing: honest, practical, explanatory.

Process: calming, organized, reassuring.

Services: specific, useful, easy to scan.

Legacy block: emotional, sincere, humble.

Areas served: local, confident, SEO-aware.

FAQ: direct, helpful, no fluff.

Contact form: reassuring, simple, low-pressure.

---

## Global Copy Rules

### Always Use

- Family-owned
- Local Cochrane team
- Clear pricing guidance
- Built for long-term value
- Neighbour-focused service
- Strong workmanship
- Safe, durable, practical
- Protect your home
- Built for the people who come after us
- Request a clear estimate

### Avoid Overusing

- Best
- Cheapest
- Luxury on every page
- World-class in customer-facing copy
- Guaranteed unless legally true
- Overhyped claims
- Vague words like quality, excellence, solutions without proof

### Replace Weak Copy With Stronger Copy

Weak: “We offer professional renovation services.”

Better: “We help Cochrane families renovate with clear pricing, careful planning, and
workmanship built to hold up years after the dust settles.”

Weak: “Contact us today.”

Better: “Tell us what you are planning. We will help you understand the scope, rough range, and
best next step.”

Weak: “High-quality construction.”

Better: “Work built carefully enough that we would be proud if our own grandchildren inherited
it.”

## Universal Copy Variables

Use these variables to adapt copy across all 115 websites.

- **[Brand Name]** = Cochrane Roofing Masters, Cochrane Tile Masters, etc.
- **[Service]** = roofing, tile installation, basement suite construction, etc.
- **[Service Category]** = exterior, interior, structural, mechanical, outdoor, commercial, etc.
- **[Primary Customer]** = homeowner, family, acreage owner, landlord, business owner, etc.
- **[Main Outcome]** = safer home, better value, more space, stronger exterior, etc.
- **[Pain Point]** = leaks, old materials, unclear pricing, bad past contractor, wasted space, etc.
- **[Community]** = Cochrane, Bearspaw, Springbank, Bragg Creek, Canmore, etc.
- **[Price Range]** = service-specific range.
- **[CTA]** = Request A Clear Estimate.

Every site should make the customer feel three things:
