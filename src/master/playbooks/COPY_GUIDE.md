# COPY GUIDE — Voice and storytelling rules across every CMB site

## Voice traits

> Populated in Phase 2 from your uploaded brand identity docs. Anchor traits to keep:
> - Calm
> - Honest
> - Local
> - Practical
> - Quietly confident — never boastful

## The unique-copy rule

**Sister sites must not share paragraphs.** Google's duplicate-content detector will sandbox you.

When remixing:
- Keep the *structure* (headline → sub → trust → CTA pattern)
- Rewrite the *words* end-to-end for the new trade
- It's fine — actually preferred — for the rhythm to stay similar

## Words to avoid

(Pulled from master brand identity in Phase 2. Examples likely to land here:)

- "luxury", "premium", "world-class"
- "best in Cochrane", "#1", "top-rated" (without proof)
- "synergy", "leverage", "best-in-class"
- "passionate about" (everyone is — say what you actually do)

## Replacements

| Avoid | Use |
|---|---|
| "premium" | "considered" / "carefully done" |
| "best" | "right" / "right-sized" |
| "passionate" | (delete; show, don't tell) |

## Story rules

Every remix must answer these questions in the hero or first scroll:
1. **What do you do?** (One specific service, not a list)
2. **For whom?** (A specific kind of homeowner with a specific problem)
3. **Where?** (Cochrane + named adjacent areas)
4. **What's the next step?** (Photos in → range out → tidy visit)

If any answer is fuzzy, the site fails the audit.

## CTAs

- Primary: "Send a couple of photos" / "Get a real range" — never "Get a quote" (too transactional, too vague)
- Secondary: "See pricing & process" — never "Learn more"

## FAQ structure

Every site keeps the same 4 questions, rewritten per trade:
1. Is my job too small?
2. How does pricing actually work?
3. How disruptive will this be?
4. Do you work outside Cochrane?
